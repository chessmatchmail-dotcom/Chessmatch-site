Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Chess Match is an app for adults. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for any content or behavior that endangers minors.

1. Adults only

  • Chess Match is for people aged 18 and over. Users must enter their date of birth when they create a profile, and the app does not let anyone under 18 create one.
  • Only accounts with a verified email address are shown to other users.
  • Users can message each other only after both have liked each other (a mutual match).

2. What is not allowed

  • Any sexual content involving minors, or any attempt to groom, solicit, exploit or endanger a minor.
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Using the app to contact, meet or target anyone under 18.

Accounts that break these rules are removed permanently, and we report them to the authorities.

3. How to report a concern

  • In the app: open any profile or chat and use Report. You can also Block or unmatch a person at any time.
  • By email: chessmatchsupport@gmail.com

4. What we do when we receive a report

  • We review reports of child safety concerns as a priority.
  • We remove the content and the account involved when a violation is found.
  • We report suspected CSAE and CSAM to the relevant authorities and to child protection organizations, as required by law, and we cooperate with their investigations.

5. Compliance with the law

We comply with applicable child safety laws and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.
