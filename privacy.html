Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Chess Match helps adults find people to play chess with in person. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have. If anything is unclear, email chessmatchsupport@gmail.com.

1. Who is responsible

Chess Match is operated by Odai Merheg, an individual based in Prague, Czech Republic ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data. Contact: chessmatchsupport@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • You must be 18 or older to use Chess Match.
  • Other users of the app can see your profile: name, age, photos, level, city, languages, bio, favorite openings, your "available now" status and an approximate distance. They never see your exact date of birth or your exact location.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads. The current version has no advertising or analytics tools.
  • You can delete your account inside the app at any time.

4. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app (contract): creating your account, showing profiles, matching, chat, game proposals and maps.
  • To keep people safe (legitimate interests and legal obligations): handling reports and blocks, enforcing the 18+ rule and our Terms, and preventing abuse.
  • Optional features (consent): location, photos and showing your zodiac sign. You can withdraw consent at any time by changing your phone permissions, editing your profile, or deleting your account.
  • To communicate with you: answering your support emails and sending important service notices.

We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other users. Profile information, photos, your "available now" status and an approximate distance. If you match with someone, they can chat with you and see the messages and game proposals you send them.
  • Our providers. Google provides Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, which store your account and app data. Our database is hosted in Google's European multi-region location. When you open a map, Google Maps (Android) or Apple Maps (iPhone) may receive data under their own privacy policies.
  • The operator. As the operator we have technical access to the database. We do not routinely read private conversations. We may review content that is reported to us.
  • Authorities. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account in the app (Profile, then Delete my account). This deletes your profile, photos, private date-of-birth record, matches, games and all messages (they disappear for the other person too), your challenges and blocks, pending chess spot suggestions, and your sign-in account. Approved chess spots stay on the map. Reports and blocks other users made about you are kept for safety and legal reasons, and backups held by our provider are overwritten after a limited time. See also how to delete your account.

7. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to access your data, correct it (you can edit your profile in the app), delete it, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy of the data you gave us, and withdraw consent. To use these rights, email chessmatchsupport@gmail.com. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, for example the Office for Personal Data Protection in the Czech Republic.

8. Children

Chess Match is only for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it. If you believe a minor is using the app, please report the profile or email us.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules in our database. No system is perfectly secure, and messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Please use a strong, unique password.

10. International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside your own. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses described in Google's terms.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will change the date above, and tell you in the app if the changes are important.
