Chess Match

Meet people near you to play chess in person.

Chess Match is an app for adults who want to find chess partners nearby, from complete beginners to experienced players. Browse profiles, challenge someone, chat after a match, and agree on a time and a public place to play.

Support

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Email chessmatchsupport@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

Report a user or a conversation

Inside the app, open the "..." menu on a profile or in a chat and choose Report or Block. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.
